Departure digital ATIS

To save as a web app on iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Not for operational use. US D-ATIS is from FAA (atis.info). Hong Kong departure ATIS is from CAD. Canadian ATIS is from NAV CANADA AeroView. Other fields are ACARS via atis.guru and may be stale — the digital copy can lag the live ATIS broadcast.

ADS-B opens an area view below 10,000 feet AGL; zoom out to show more aircraft in the area.

The author assumes no responsibility. Opens are counted as one number; no identity, airport, or location is stored.
