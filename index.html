GearUp

FLIGHT INFORMATION

    To save as a web app on iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Not for operational use. US D-ATIS is from FAA (atis.info). Hong Kong departure ATIS is from CAD. Canadian ATIS is from NAV CANADA AeroView. Other fields are overheard ACARS (Airframes, with atis.guru as a fallback) and may be stale — the digital copy can lag the live ATIS broadcast. GearUp always opens on departure ATIS; ARR shows an arrival copy when one was overheard.
    EHAM, Amsterdam has more features such as flight board and slot information.

    ADS-B opens an area view below 10,000 feet AGL; zoom out to show more aircraft in the area.
