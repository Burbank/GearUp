GearUp Privacy and Support

GearUp is a free, unofficial airport briefing app for education only.
Not for operational use.

We do not collect personal data, run analytics, or show ads.
Pins and theme stay on your device.

When you look up an airport, GearUp asks its own service for briefing information.
That comes from official data providers and open-source sources.

Ordinary web logs such as time and IP may exist on the host.
No account is created, and your activity is not logged or tracked.

Some screens open a third-party map or airport page.
Those pages have their own terms.
